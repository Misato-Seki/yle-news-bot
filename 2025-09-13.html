Fatal shooting by police in Espoo

https://yle.fi/a/74-20182746

2025-09-13

A man died as a result of police gunfire in Espoo on Saturday, the Western Uusimaa Police Department said. According to a statement on Saturday morning, officers "had to use a firearm". There were unsuccessful attempts to resuscitate the person who was shot, it said. A preliminary investigation will be initiated into the use of weapons by the police in accordance with the Criminal Investigation Act. Since the turn of the millennium, 14 people have died in Finland due to police use of force.

Orpo on airspace violations: No direct threat to Finland

https://yle.fi/a/74-20182736

2025-09-13

Prime MinisterPetteri Orpo said Russia does not intend to escalate its war in Ukraine. He spoke on Yle's Ykkösaamu talk show after Russia flew about 20 drones deep into Polish airspace on Wednesday. Orpo hopes the airspace violation will be a turning point in the West's attitude towards Russia. He hopes it will lead to stricter sanctions against Moscow and increased military support for Ukraine.
