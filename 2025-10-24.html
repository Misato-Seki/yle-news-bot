Finland sees decrease in elk-related car crashes, but accident black spots remain

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190197

2025-10-24

Last year, there were about 1,666 elk-related crashes on Finnish roads. This is a significant decrease on the figure of over 2,000 in 2017. The areas around Kouvola and Mikkeli, for example, saw 164 and 156 crashes last year. The country's elk population has decreased from over 100,000 individuals at the end of the 2010s to just over 80,000 this year.

Finnish entrepreneurs more optimistic about the future

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190176

2025-10-24

Economic outlook for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) is the second-best ever, according to a survey by the Federation of Finnish Enterprises. In a membership poll published on Friday, 49 percent of firms said that their financial situation is very or fairly good. This marks an improvement since the previous survey in August. The most bullish outlooks were among companies offering specialist services and firms employing more than 10 people.
