Kela launches subsidised private medical access for over-65s

https://yle.fi/a/74-20180402

2025-09-01

Finland's experiment with 'freedom' in healthcare begins on Monday, 1 September. Under the programme, people over 65 may see a private practitioner while paying a public healthcare fee. The trial, scheduled to run until the end of 2027, allows for a maximum of two visits this year and up to three annually thereafter. It excludes specialist consultations and cannot be used for administrative medical checks, such as those required for a driver's licence.
