PM: Ukraine seeing positive development

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190474

2025-10-26

Prime MinisterPetteri Orpo says there are signs of positive development in the war in Ukraine. He noted in particular a tougher stance toward Moscow from US PresidentDonald Trump. Orpo said Russia's advance on the front had stalled, while Ukraine's capacity to strike targets inside Russia had improved. He described as highly significant China's decision, prompted by US sanctions, to limit purchases of Russian oil, noting that China has been one of Moscow's largest buyers.

Border guard intercepts two at Finland's eastern frontier

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190452

2025-10-26

North Karelia Border Guard is investigating a suspected illegal border crossing. On Saturday evening, guards detained two foreign nationals suspected of entering Finland from Russia on foot. A local hunter spotted the two on a road, gave them a lift, and notified emergency services. Border Guard officers intercepted them roughly 20 kilometres from the border. Both have applied for asylum in Finland. The border with Russia has been closed for nearly two years.

Health experts want flu shots in daycares to boost coverage

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190442

2025-10-26

Only a third of young children in Finland get the seasonal flu vaccine. Coverage among 0–6-year-olds ranges from 13 percent in some areas to 43 percent in others. Coverage is lowest in Ostrobothnia and highest in South Karelia and North Savo. For maximum benefit, the jab should be administered annually, ideally before the flu season begins, in November or December.

Furry paws take Finland's nature photo of the year

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190438

2025-10-26

Ossi Saarinen's winning photograph offers a close-up study of a lynx's thick-furred paws. The photograph only shows the animal's sturdy forelegs and furry paws resting on a moss-covered tree trunk. Organised by the Finnish Nature Photographers’ Association, the competition drew more than 12,000 entries from over 700 photographers. This year marked its 45th edition.
