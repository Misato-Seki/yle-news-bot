Finland joins new EU border entry/exit system

https://yle.fi/a/74-20188118

2025-10-13

The EU began rolling out a new Entry/Exit System (EES) for non-EU nationals from Sunday 12 October. EES is an automated IT system that will eventually replace the traditional stamping of passports at EU border checkpoints. The system is part of a broader effort to strengthen the security of the Schengen area's external borders. The British Embassy in Helsinki released a video on its social media platforms over the weekend to advise UK citizens about how the changes will affect them.

USU: Finnish MPs to practice using defence shelter under Parliament House

https://yle.fi/a/74-20188039

2025-10-13

Finnish MPs will practice sheltering in basement of Parliament House later this year. The drill will mark the first time that Finnish lawmakers have practised using the civil defence shelter under the parliament building. The shelter includes a backup plenary hall, where parliamentary sessions can be held in exceptional circumstances, such as during wartime. Parliament's First Deputy SpeakerPaula Risikko(NCP) told USU that the exercise is part of broader efforts to step up preparations.
