Foreign students' need for social assistance rises sharply since last year

https://yle.fi/a/74-20182776

2025-09-14

More than 12 million euros in social assistance was paid to foreign-language students during the first eight months of this year. Nearly 40 percent of students receiving financial support are foreign- language speakers. A legislative reform in 2022 made it easier for students from outside Europe to enter the country. Interior Minister Mari Rantanen said that the reform should be reversed, due to students’ growing financial problems.

US missile destroyer arrives in Finland as Nato beefs up eastern flank

https://yle.fi/a/74-20182806

2025-09-14

The USS Bainbridge docked at the Hernesaari quay on Sunday morning. The guided-missile destroyer specialises in anti-air, anti-surface and anti-submarine warfare. It is best known for its role in the 2009 hijacking of a cargo ship off the coast of Somalia. In 2013, the story was made into the filmCaptain Phillips, starring Tom Hanks, which earned six Oscar nominations.
