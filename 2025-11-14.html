Finnish study links severe morning sickness with increased depression risk

https://yle.fi/a/74-20194212

2025-11-14

Study at the University of Turku found link between severe morning sickness and depression. Women who suffered severemorning sickness were more likely to suffer from depression before and after their pregnancies. They were also diagnosed with post-partum depression earlier. Severe morning sickness involves serious nausea and vomiting that prevents normal eating and drinking, making it difficult to cope with everyday life. The study's findings were based on examining medical data on more than 437,000 women from registries spanning the years 2004-2015.
