Investigation concludes but mystery remains over mass seal death off Finland's south coast

https://yle.fi/a/74-20176484

2025-08-08

The bodies of about 20 seals were found in the sea along Finland's southeastern coast. The cause of their deaths remains unknown. An autopsy on one of the dead seals ruled out common causes such as bird flu. The City of Porvoo has brought the investigation to an end, meaning the reason behind the mass death will remain a mystery. The investigation was also unable to determine when the seals might have died.

Experts say Purra's plans to abolish integration funding will increase segregation, further weaken job prospects

https://yle.fi/a/74-20176480

2025-08-08

Finland's finance minister plans to abolish funding aimed at helping immigrants integrate into Finnish society. Current system involves municipalities and regional healthcare authorities providing services for immigrants, and then claiming that money back from the state. People who avail of these services include quota refugees, asylum seekers and victims of human trafficking. City of Helsinki's own estimates: this year it will spend about 17 million euros on integration funding.
