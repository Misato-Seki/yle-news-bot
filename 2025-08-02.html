Finland’s chief meteorologist sees climate change impact from his summer cottage on Lake Saimaa

https://yle.fi/a/74-20175607

2025-08-02

Finland's climate is warming significantly faster than the global average. Last year, the increase in the average global temperature exceeded the critical threshold of 1.5 degrees Celsius. In Finland, last year was 3.4 degrees warmer than pre-industrial levels. Temperatures in various parts of Finland have risen to levels that are dangerously high for trout and Arctic grayling. Climate change is making Finnish winters milder and wetter.

TS: Timo Soini weighs political comeback with Centre Party

https://yle.fi/a/74-20175590

2025-08-02

Timo Soini, long-time chair of the Finns Party, is considering a return to politics after a four-year hiatus. Soini told the newspaper Turun Sanomat that if he does, it would be as a candidate for the opposition Centre Party. The Centre has the potential to aim for the prime ministerial post after the spring 2027 elections, Soini said. He said that he has no interest in establishing a new party.

Survey: Finnish companies still eager to enter US market despite tariff threat

https://yle.fi/a/74-20175576

2025-08-02

Survey: 75 percent of Finnish companies are considering increasing or starting exports to EU countries. 28 percent are also considering increasing exports to the USA and Canada. Companies are clearly less interested in exports to Southeast Asia and China. US consumers are seeing higher prices for imported products due to tariffs and a weaker dollar. "Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) are facing a really challenging situation," says Harri Jaskari, Director of the Finnish Entrepreneurs' Association.
