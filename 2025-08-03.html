Two killed as van and train collide in Siilinjärvi

https://yle.fi/a/74-20175694

2025-08-03

A man and a woman in their late 60s died in the crash in Siilinjärvi, eastern Finland. The deceased were Finnish citizens who lived abroad. The accident occurred at an unguarded level crossing on Ruuskalantie, when the van drove into it from the direction of National Road 5 (also known as E63) The train stayed on the tracks during the accident and all passengers were safe.

28 drownings in July

https://yle.fi/a/74-20175670

2025-08-03

The number of drowning deaths in Finland soared to 28 in July, a sharp increase from last year. That is according to preliminary statistics from the Finnish Swimming Teaching and Lifesaving Federation (FSL) Sixteen of the drownings occurred while swimming. Eight people drowned in water traffic. The details of four other cases remain unclear. July is typically the month with the highest number of drownings, according to the FSL.

Finnish schools re-open this week with new laws and reforms in place

https://yle.fi/a/74-20175620

2025-08-03

Minister of Education Anders Adlercreutz warns municipalities that extra funding earmarked to improve learning at schools must be spent on that and not diverted to other expenditures. Nearly all Finnish comprehensive schools re-open this coming week, with most starting classes on Wednesday or Thursday. The academic year begins amidst changes, with several government reforms coming into effect at the same time. The primary goal of all of them is to combat the decline in learning outcomes among youngsters in Finland.
