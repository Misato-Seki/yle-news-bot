Finnish mothers labour two hours more than 20 years ago

https://yle.fi/a/74-20176693

2025-08-10

Twenty years ago, labour from the start of cervical dilation to the end of the pushing stage lasted on average just under eight hours. Today, delivery takes nearly ten hours. Experts say lengthening of labour could be linked to both changes in delivery practices as well as changes in mothers. In 2024, the average age of mothers giving birth in Finland was 31.9, with women aged 35 and over accounting for 27.7 percent of deliveries.
