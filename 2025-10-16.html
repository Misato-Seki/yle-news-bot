Parliament approves further tightening of Finland's citizenship laws

https://yle.fi/a/74-20188729

2025-10-16

The motion was passed by 103 votes to 58 at a plenary session on Wednesday. In future, applicants must be able to prove they can support themselves through employment or entrepreneurship. Previously, social welfare payments were also accepted as a source of income when applying for Finnish citizenship — but they will not be in future. The reform also sets a higher bar for an applicant's "integrity", meaning committing offences or crimes will have a more substantial impact on whether they can be granted citizenship.

Evictions on the rise in Finland, often tied to drug use

https://yle.fi/a/74-20188707

2025-10-16

Most common reason for eviction is still the non-payment of rent. Experts say that the number of serious incidents has increased, often related to the use of intoxicants, especially drugs. The number of evictions in the largest cities has increased by an average of 80 percent over the past decade. There are now over a million households living in rented premises, and a large number of new rental apartments have been completed in recent years.

Eight percent of children in vehicles found not using seat belts, car seats

https://yle.fi/a/74-20188702

2025-10-16

Joint traffic inspections were carried out at seven locations near daycare centres at the end of September. The majority of drivers with children in their cars were doing so safely, but the inspections also found serious shortfalls and deficiencies in the condition of the child seats. Traffic laws state that children riding in vehicles must be properly secured in child seats until they are at least 135cm tall. The law also says that kids under the age of three are not permitted to travel in passenger cars, vans or trucks without being appropriately secured.
