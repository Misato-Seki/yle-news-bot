More than 10k who fled Russia's war in Ukraine have earned wages in Finland

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185842

2025-09-30

At the end of 2024, just over 10,000 people who fled the war in Ukraine had earned income in Finland. Those workers were primarily employed in the agricultural sector, the forestry and fisheries industry. The most common type of jobs they held were as cleaners and agricultural sector assistants. However, precisely estimating how many people there are in Finland receiving temporary protection is difficult, as many leave the country without notifying authorities.
