Finland backs Nokia-led plan for AI gigafactory

https://yle.fi/a/74-20171278

2025-07-06

Nokia has stepped forward with an expression of interest to bring such a facility to Finland. The proposed factories would have four times the computing power of current AI centres. The European Commission’s InvestAI initiative is targeting 200 billion euros’ worth of investments in AI and high-performance computing (HPC) The government bills Finland as "an ideal location for an AI gigafactory, largely due to clean energy grids and land availability"

German firm suspected of illegal alcohol sales in Finland, avoiding €40m in taxes

https://yle.fi/a/74-20171236

2025-07-06

Two German men and a Finnish-born woman are suspected of aggravated tax fraud and aggravated alcohol-related offences. Based on the preliminary investigation, the German firm evaded nearly 40 million euros in excise duties and value-added taxes due to Finnish authorities. The firm "gained a very significant criminal benefit and a significant competitive advantage over others operating legally in the same market," chief investigatorJuha Havumäkisaid.

Three Tampere stabbing victims released from hospital

https://yle.fi/a/74-20171230

2025-07-06

Three of the four victims of Thursday’s stabbings in central Tampere have been released from the hospital. One of the three women who sustained injuries in the stabbing was still hospitalised on Saturday evening. According to Detective InspectorOssi Kaario, her life is not in danger. Police are investigating Thursday's violence as four cases of attempted murder. The suspect is a 23-year-old Finnish man with a record of violent crimes.
