North Savo ends contracts with foreign nurses, doctors over language, skills gaps

https://yle.fi/a/74-20172763

2025-07-15

10 nurses and two doctors, all of foreign backgrounds, were hired just last year. North Savo healthcare authority said their professional and language skills were not up to the expected standard. The workers had been stationed at inpatient wards in the towns of Iisalmi and Varkaus, as well as in the city of Kuopio. The county said the recruitment agency did not carry out sufficiently thorough checks into the workers' skill and language levels.

FMI issues heat warning for this week

https://yle.fi/a/74-20172674

2025-07-15

The Finnish Meteorological Institute (FMI) has issued a warning for hot weather across Finland until Friday. The warning covers nearly the entire country, except for Central and Northern Lapland as well as the Åland islands. FMI issues potentially hazardous 'hot weather' warnings when the daily high temperature exceeds 27 degrees Celsius and the average temperature is at least 20 degrees. The summer’s temperature record was broken on Monday in Kaskinen, Ostrobothnia, where the mercury reached 32.4 degrees.

EVA survey: Rising number of Finns lean politically right

https://yle.fi/a/74-20172659

2025-07-15

Nearly half of Finns (49%) identify with the political right. A third (31%) position themselves on the left. Only one in five (19%) now identify as the political centre. The proportion of people identifying as right-wing is now at its highest level in the history of EVA's surveys. The survey is based on responses from 2,070 participants aged between 17 and 89.
