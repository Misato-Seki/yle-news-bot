Wolt plans trial to hire some couriers as employees

https://yle.fi/a/74-20187152

2025-10-07

Wolt is starting a trial to hire some of its couriers as employees. Wolt said it plans to hire around 100 couriers in the coming months. The firm currently uses the services of around 10-thousand couriers. Earlier this year, theSupreme Administrative Court ruled that food couriers are employees, not self-employed entrepreneurs. The company is in discussions with the service sector workers' union PAM about setting up a collective agreement.

Finland's Immigration Service now accepting Palestinian travel documents

https://yle.fi/a/74-20187074

2025-10-07

Finnish Immigration Service (Migri) has started accepting travel documents issued by the Palestinian Authority. Migri has not accepted passports issued by Palestinian Authority, because Finland has not recognised the state of Palestine. Following a review of the Aliens Act and Citizenship Act, Migri said it considered legal obligations to not restrict the rights of foreign nationals "any more than necessary" Migri added that it plans to further examine how the guideline changes end up affecting citizenship and residence permit applications.

2025-10-07

Children in Finland are increasingly starting daycare at earlier stages in their lives. Despite the country's shrinking birthrate, the overall use of early childhood education services is rising. Boosting daycare participation rates was mentioned in Prime MinisterPetteri Orpo's (NCP) government programme. According to the Finnish National Agency for Education, around 43 percent of children under the age of two were in daycare last year.
