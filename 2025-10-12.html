Daycare centres gain popularity in Finland as homestyle family daycare becomes a rarity

https://yle.fi/a/74-20187949

2025-10-12

Family daycare, which is arranged in child minders' own homes, was popular in Finland in the 1980s, but appears to be fading away. Now only about three percent of children in daycare are in such small-group care. Children in Finland typically start school around age seven, but receive some pre-school education before that. Pre-primary education is free of charge and has been compulsory for all children since 2015.

Ministry: 65 wolves in the crosshairs this winter

https://yle.fi/a/74-20187938

2025-10-12

Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry plans to allow hunting of at least 65 wolves this coming winter. The ministry has decided a wolf population of 273 would ensure a favourable conservation status, far below the current population of about 430. Wolves prey on livestock and pets, but do not pose a serious direct threat to humans. Meanwhile elk hunting season began throughout the country on Saturday. The Finnish Wildlife Agency has issued over 38,000Elk hunting permits for this season.
