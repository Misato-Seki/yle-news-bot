More than half of Finland's employees say they work remotely on vacation

https://yle.fi/a/74-20173591

2025-07-21

More than half of respondents in Finland said they work while on vacation. Digitalisation of working life has turned vacation periods into a new arena for work. Only about 15 percent of respondents said they were able to completely disconnect from work. Nearly 90 percent of men said they checked work messages or emails at least occasionally during their holidays, while slightly fewer female respondents said the same. The survey queried around 1,000 residents in Finland between the ages of 18 and 75.

2025-07-21

Sami Asujamaa was surprised to learn that the National Enforcement Authority Finland had seized money from his company's account to settle an unpaid invoice. Thousands of unjustified, outdated, or unreasonable payment claims are made against ordinary consumers each year through what is known as summary civil cases. The FCCA is proposing several changes to the current summary debt collection process. Hot July causes spike in drownings and dangerous near-misses

https://yle.fi/a/74-20173538

2025-07-21

As of 20 July, seven people have drowned in Finland this month. In June, drownings in Finland were significantly lower than last year — eight this year compared to 23 in the same period in 2024. The Finnish Swimming Teaching and Lifesaving Federation attributes the increase to hot weather and the busy holiday season. Most drownings occurred in western and inland Finland, regions known for their popular cottage destinations.
