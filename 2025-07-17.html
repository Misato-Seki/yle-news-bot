Finns trust politicians less, survey finds

https://yle.fi/a/74-20173101

2025-07-17

Only one percent of Finland's population say they have "very high" trust in politicians. The poll found that just 10 percent of respondents expressed strong trust, while nearly half reported little or no confidence in political leaders. The results mirror sentiments in Norway, but trust remains significantly higher in neighbouring Sweden and Denmark. The Norstat Quarterly Data survey was conducted in 16 European countries and received responses from 1,016 Finland-based residents and over 4,000 people across the Nordics in June.
