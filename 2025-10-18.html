MPs cool on state funding for news agency STT

https://yle.fi/a/74-20189113

2025-10-18

Few parliamentary groups support using public funds to prop up STT, the Finnish News Agency. Only the Greens clearly back the idea, while the ruling National Coalition Party and the Christian Democrats favour a market-based solution. The Finns Party has not adopted an official position, but its parliamentary group leader,Jani Mäkelä, opposes direct state funding for STT. STT's largest shareholder, Sanoma Media Finland, has said it plans to reduce its use of the agency's news and photo services significantly.

Boy who crossed from Finland into Russia returns to Ukraine

https://yle.fi/a/74-20189079

2025-10-18

13-year-old Ukrainian boy who reportedly walked across Finland's eastern borderinto Russia in January has returned to Ukraine, reports news agency Reuters. Finnish media reported in February that a teenager had crossed the border on foot near the town of Imatra, at the end of January, before being detained by Russian authorities. Yle has not been able to verify whether the boy is the same individual who made headlines earlier this year, as Finnish officials have not confirmed the case.

Veikkaus faces scrutiny over social media promotions

https://yle.fi/a/74-20189074

2025-10-18

Veikkaus, Finland's state-owned gambling monopoly, has been buying visibility on social media for posts about its casino and slot-machine games. The company denied violating Finland's lottery law. Finland'sgambling system is set for an overhaulin 2027, when online betting will shift to a licensing model, effectively ending Veikkauses' monopoly. The National Police Board told STT that sharing information about gambling locations is permitted.
