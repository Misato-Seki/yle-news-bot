Saimaa ringed seal recognised as distinct species

https://yle.fi/a/74-20173801

2025-07-22

The Saimaa ringed seal was previously classified as a subspecies. It is Finland’s only endemic mammal, meaning it exists nowhere else in the world. An estimated 500 seals live in Lake SaimAA in Eastern Finland. Thanks to conservation efforts, the seals' population has grown slowly, but they remain vulnerable to environmental changes and human-related harms. The seal's scientific name will change toPusa saimensis, replacing the formerPusa hispida saimensi.

2025-07-22

There were 27,000 more unemployed people in April–June than in the same period last year. As unemployment rose, the number of employed people fell in the second quarter.
