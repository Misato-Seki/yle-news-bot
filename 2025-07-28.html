Utsjoki's summertime 'nightless nights' end after two months of daylight

https://yle.fi/a/74-20174750

2025-07-28

The Sun is due to set in Utsjoki, Finnish Lapland, on Monday night. It is still relatively bright all night long as sunrise and sunset occur when the geometric center of the sun dips half a degree below the horizon. The period of daylight hours in southern areas lasts for more than two-and-a-half months. The final night of full darkness along the southern coast of Finland was on 11 May.

Covid prompted more home schooling — now the trend continues

https://yle.fi/a/74-20174703

2025-07-28

In 2020, 585 children were being home-schooled in Finland. Last year, the total of kids in Finland being homeschooled rose to 881. Finnish law allows parents to choose homeschooling without special permission. However, legislation offers only limited guidance on how the pedagogy should be regulated or monitored, leaving those responsibilities to local municipalities. Finland has laws regarding compulsory education, but where kids are taught is more open to interpretation.
