International families lukewarm on Finland's plans to delay school summer holidays

https://yle.fi/a/74-20178503

2025-08-20

Education minister Anders Adlercreutz wants Finnish schools to start their summer holidays in the middle of June. He argues that this would be a win-win for Finnish businesses as well as for Finnish families. But parents say the proposal will not 'solve' the childcare challenges parents face in June — but will merely move them to later in the summer. Parents also say that scheduling school holidays to run from mid-June to mid-August could have the unintended consequence of driving up flight and accommodation costs for families.

Security worries delay Finns' plans to start families

https://yle.fi/a/74-20178466

2025-08-20

A majority of young Finns said they believe that Finland's security situation will deteriorate in the coming years. One in three young people reported postponing or abandoning their plans to have children due to concerns about the global security environment. Women most often called for increased diplomacy and efforts to ensure peace. Men, on the other hand, most often favoured bolstering the European Union's common defence.

2025-08-20

At the end of June, 57 percent of all unemployed were classified as structurally unemployed. In June, more than a fifth — 22 percent — of 15–24-year-olds in the labour force were unemployed. The situation is especially dire for young people lacking both work experience and formal qualifications.
