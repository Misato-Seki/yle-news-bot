Study: Over half of Finns are open to care from medical professionals learning Finnish or Swedish

https://yle.fi/a/74-20178487

2025-08-21

More than half of Finns are willing to receive care from doctors and nurses who are still learning Finnish or Swedish. Two-thirds of respondents said they are happy to speak a foreign language, even if their skills are imperfect. Finnish identity is important to nearly everyone, with around 85 percent of respondents saying they want to be understood in their mother tongue. Around 70 percent consider proficiency in standard language a mark of education and want standard Finnish to be used in official contexts.
