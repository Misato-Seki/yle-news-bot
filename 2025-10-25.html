HS: Porvoo teacher realises teens can't tell time

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190394

2025-10-25

A significant number of lower secondary students (grades 7-9) at the Albert Edelfeltin school in Porvoo are unable to read an analogue clock. This was reported byHelsingin Sanomat, which wrote that many teenagers were unable to complete clock-reading exercises designed for second graders. The growing influence of English among young people may be confusing their understanding of how time is expressed in Finnish and Swedish.

Supply of foreign care workers dries up

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190371

2025-10-25

Only 32 applicants, mostly from the Philippines and Sri Lanka, received their first residence permit to work in health and social care. During the same period last year, the agency granted more than 570 such work permits. The slowdown marks a sharp reversal from 2023, a record year in which more than 2,000 health- and social-care workers received residence permits. Nursing union Tehy has said planned redundancies are impacting international recruitment.

Finland turns back the clock

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190358

2025-10-25

This weekend, Finland will move from daylight saving to standard time. The ritual, performed annually on the last Sunday of October, ushers in roughly five months of wintertime. A majority of respondents to an EU survey said they would like to end the clock changes. Ending the tradition would require a qualified majority in the Council, meaning at least 15 member states would have to back the proposal.
