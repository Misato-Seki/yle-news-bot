Finland plans to ease rules on firing workers

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190092

2025-10-23

Government proposes to make it easier for employers to dismiss staff. A "proper reason" would suffice for terminating an employment contract. Current law requires the reason to be both proper and weighty. Not every instance of reproachable behaviour or inadequate performance would constitute sufficient grounds for dismissal, the government said in astatement. Taking parental leave would not, for example, constitute grounds for dismissing.

Bears out, seals in? Regional boss leads calls to change Finland's national animal

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190053

2025-10-23

Heini Utunen, the regional director of South Savo, wants the Saimaa ringed seal to replace the brown bear as Finland's national animal. The bear was chosen as the national animal in 1985 following a public vote organised by newspaper Helsingin Sanomat alongside the Finnish Association for Nature Conservation. The seal came in second in the poll, with the council thanking them for increasing awareness of the species.

Finland sees rise in birth rate during 2025

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190044

2025-10-23

Some 1,300 more children were born by the end of September than during the same period last year. Finland's preliminary total fertility rate of 1.28 exceeds the levels seen in 2023 and 2024. But even with this uptick, the rate is the third-lowest in a 250-year series, Statistics Finland says. At the same time, migration into Finland is slowing, according to preliminary data.

Nokia's profit slips, Elisa trims jobs

2025-10-23

Finnish telecommunications giant Nokia reported a 10 percent drop in comparable operating profit for the third quarter. The firm said that operating profit fell to 435 million euros in July–September, down from 485 million a year earlier. Nokia's sales grew 12 percent year-on-year to 4.8 billion euros, from 4.3 billion. Finnish telecoms company Elisa announced plans to cut up to 450 jobs worldwide, of which around 400 will be in Finland.

Google's plans to expand its data centre infrastructure in Finland are now on hold, according to areport by Helsingin Sanomat.
