Orpo also expressed satisfaction that Trump had spoken with Ukrainian PresidentVolodymyr Zelensky immediately after meeting with Putin. Passengers evacuated from plane after accident at Helsinki Airport

https://yle.fi/a/74-20177819

2025-08-16

The propeller of a grounded Finnair passenger aircraft struck a ground support vehicle. Both the vehicle and the aircraft sustained damage, but no one was injured in the collision. The 68 people on board were evacuated from the plane and taken back to the terminal. Airport operator Finavia said later that Finnair flight AY1175 to Gdansk had been cancelled. There were slight delays in some early-afternoon European arrivals and departures.

Putin-Trump meeting: "Worst-case scenario didn't happen"

https://yle.fi/a/74-20177813

2025-08-16

Russian President Vladimir Putin met with his US counterpart,Donald Trump, in Anchorage late Friday Finnish time. The talks were aimed at ending Russia's war of aggression in Ukraine – or at least a ceasefire. The two held a brief press conference afterwards, but did not announce any significant agreements or take questions from reporters. The worst-case scenarios did not come true, Sinikukka Saari, a Leading Researcher at the Finnish Institute of International Affairs said.
