2025-09-27

40 organisations representing Finland's Muslim community have sharply criticised the discussion around whether face coverings or headscarves should be banned from public places. The statement added that the Muslim community has followed the debate with "great concern and disappointment" The latest iteration of the long-running debate was sparked last month by a post on social media by Finland's Social Security MinisterSanni Grahn-Laasonen.

STT: Ukrainian workers underpaid, overworked at Turku shipyard

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185331

2025-09-27

Ukrainian workers at Turku's shipyard say they have not been paid a significant amount of wages they are due. Investigation by Finnish news agency STT uncovered evidence that the Ukrainians were pressured into working on weekends, at night, and in shifts that lasted longer than eight hours. The shipyard operator, Meyer Turku, told STT that it is aware of the matter, but did not wish to comment on whether the Ukrainian workers were underpaid.

Finnish woman's Lego-built wedding dress goes on display in Denmark

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185325

2025-09-27

Satu Aaltonen is only the second Finn in history to receive an invitation to the Masterpiece Gallery exhibition in Denmark. Her creation is a wearable wedding dress, called theBride of the Frozen Crown, and it was built from thousands of Lego bricks. "I wanted to create something out of Lego that has not been seen before," she said. Eero Okkonen is the only other Finn to have been invited to display their works.

Sunny weekend in store for Finland

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185321

2025-09-27

This weekend will be warm and sunny across Finland, according to Yle meteorologist Elias Paakkanen. Temperatures on both Saturday and Sunday are expected to climb as high as 17 degrees Celsius. Although the days will be warmer, the nights will be cold, with temperatures dropping to between 0 and 5 degrees Celsius in western areas. Paak Kanen added that the sunny weather will continue into next week, although temperatures are forecast to drop slightly on Monday.
