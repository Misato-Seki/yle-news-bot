Finland's average unemployment duration reaches record length

https://yle.fi/a/74-20172488

2025-07-14

This May marked a bleak milestone:the average Finnish jobseeker had been unemployed for 70 weeks. 20–24-year-olds were unemployed for an average of 34 weeks, while those aged 60–64 faced job searches lasting over 100 weeks. The number of people unemployed for over two years is now at an eight-year high. The share of people out of work for more than five years has also reached a record level.
