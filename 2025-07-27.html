Underground 'lake' helps cool Helsinki as demand for district cooling rises

https://yle.fi/a/74-20174567

2025-07-27

Helsinki has a district cooling system that provides both heating and cooling. At the heart of the cooling system is a massive, 25-million-litre cold water reservoir. As of the end of 2024, about 550 buildings are part of the system. Nationwide, 13 companies offered district cooling in 2023, with a total network length of around 200 kilometres. However, smaller cities are beginning to adopt the technology. Seinäjoki is set to open its first district cooling pipeline this year.

Finland's record heat streak extends to 16 days

https://yle.fi/a/74-20174549

2025-07-27

Sunday marks the 16th consecutive day where temperatures have reached above 30 degrees Celsius. This marks the third day in a row that the all-time record has been surpassed. Another heat record was also broken Saturday in Finnish Lapland, where temperatures of over 25 degrees have now been recorded for 17 days. The highest temperatures are expected in the eastern parts of the country, where conditions could once again approach or exceed 30 degrees.

Divers find body of third drowned teenager in Kalajoki

https://yle.fi/a/74-20174534

2025-07-27

A third teenager who went missing during a tragic drowning incident in Kalajoki on Friday has been found dead, Finnish police confirmed late Saturday night. The boy, born in 2008, was located around 11pm with the help of sonar imaging, which revealed a possible sighting of a body underwater. Authorities said the body was found close to where the other two teenagers — both born in 2009 — were recovered on Friday, but at a greater depth of around three metres.
