Private healthcare provider Pihlajalinna begins redundancy talks

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185039

2025-09-25

Pihlajalinna, one of Finland's largest private providers of social and healthcare services, has announced the beginning of extensive restructuring negotiations. The company said that the talks will affect the entire workforce of 6,500 employees and could lead to about 90 people losing their jobs. The redundancies are likely to mostly affect administrative staff, the company said, and would not include doctors and nurses who work exclusively in clinical healthcare roles.

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185006

2025-09-25

Finnish airport operator Finavia said airports in Finland are functioning as usual. Drone flights near airports are banned without special permission, and Finland's transport regulator, Traficom, has set protective buffer zones around them. The drones spotted over Denmark appear to be part of a coordinated operation, according to a senior researcher at the Finnish Institute of International Affairs. Earlier this month, Poland shot down Russian drones after an airspace violation.

StatFin: Foreign tourist visits to Finland returning to pre-pandemic levels

https://yle.fi/a/74-20184973

2025-09-25

Overnight stays by foreign tourists in Finland during the summer months was 13 percent higher compared to the same period last year. This puts the number of overnight stays this year — estimated to be a total of around 760,000 — at about the same level as in the summer of 2019, just before the Covid pandemic. On average, tourists paid about 121 euros for a night in a hotel room in Finland this summer. The highest average price was in the Pirkanmaa region.

2025-09-25

Finland has been one of the most successful countries in the EU at creating integration policies for immigrants, according to an EU-wide study. Thursday's papers: Uncertainty for Ukrainians, targeted tax breaks, and Finnish gambling's last spin

https://yle.fi/a/74-20184906

2025-09-25

Ukrainians who fled to Finland after Russia's invasion of their country face an uncertain future as their temporary protection status is due to expire in March 2027. Years spent under temporary protection in Finland will not count toward the residency requirement for a permanent residence permit. Finland's gambling system is set for an overhaul in 2027, when online betting shifts to a licensing model. The reform will end Veikkaus' monopoly and channel tax revenue into state coffers.
