E-scooter accidents barely decline despite Finland's tougher laws

https://yle.fi/a/74-20180671

2025-09-02

There were almost the same number of accidents involving electric scooters this past July as a year earlier, around 200. Finland's e-scooter laws were tightened in June, which led to the lowering of the blood alcohol content (BAC) limit to 0.5g/l, and banning their use by anyone under the age of 15. Two fatal accidents have been reported this year, in Joensuu and Pirkkala.

Inflation rises slightly in Finland, but so does consumer spending power

https://yle.fi/a/74-20180582

2025-09-02

Finland's inflation rose to an annual rate of 2.2 percent last month, edging up from two percent in July. On an annual basis, the largest increases were in the areas of communications and health, where prices have risen by approximately 6.7 percent. On a monthly basis, price tags for clothing and footwear increased the most in August, rising nearly 1.5 percent just as many families were buying back-to-school gear.
