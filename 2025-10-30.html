Crackdown on kebab, pizza & Chinese restaurants finds €11m in unpaid taxes

https://yle.fi/a/74-20191372

2025-10-30

Tax Administration says it uncovered 11 million euros in unpaid taxes during stepped-up audits of restaurants over the past two years. During that time, approximately 370 restaurant companies were inspected – mainly pizza, kebab and Chinese eateries. In almost 200 cases, or some 53 percent of establishments that were probed, such serious problems were found that they were handed over for possible criminal prosecution. A similar programme that concluded in 2022 found nearly20 million eurosin unpaid taxes in the restaurant sector over the previous five years.

Potential home sellers hold out for higher prices, study finds

https://yle.fi/a/74-20191330

2025-10-30

People aiming to sell their homes often leave them unsold if the market price is below the price they originally paid. That's according to a joint study by the Vatt Institute for Economic Research and the University of Helsinki. The likelihood of people selling their homes and moving drops sharply when anticipated capital gains from a sale would result in losses. Because of the decline in inter-urban migration, the study found households were more likely to live in housing that is poorly suited to their situation.

Finnair profit drops by 30%, mostly due to strikes

https://yle.fi/a/74-20191243

2025-10-30

Finnair's comparable operating profit fell to approximately 51 million euros in July–September of this year. That was down by around 30 percent from a year earlier, when the carrier posted a profit of around 72 million euros. Finnair cancelled over 1,200 flights during the spring and summer due to industrial action by the Finnish Aviation Union (IAU) That labour dispute was finallyresolvedin mid-July after dragging on for more than half a year.
