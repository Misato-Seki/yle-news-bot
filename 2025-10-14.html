Deputy justice chancellor raps police for unlawfully ending dozens of domestic violence investigations

https://yle.fi/a/74-20188287

2025-10-14

Deputy Justice Chancellor Mikko Puumalainen examined 65 cases classified as domestic violence criminal investigations. He found that dozens of the cases had been closed because the victims did not press charges or demand that the perpetrators be legally penalised. Domestic violence is a crime subject to prosecution, regardless of whether the victim wishes the case to proceed. Because of this, domestic violence investigations should not be closed on such grounds.
