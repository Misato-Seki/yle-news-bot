Finland not recognising Palestine at UN next week

https://yle.fi/a/74-20183761

2025-09-18

Prime MinisterPetteri Orpo(NCP) said the government will not push a proposal to recognise Palestine before next week's UN General Assembly (GA) meeting. Orpo noted that the GA meeting is not a deadline for recognition. He stressed that the most urgent priorities now are to stop the killing in Gaza, to end breaches of international law and to deliver humanitarian aid. He also highlighted that an important step toward a two-state solution is thedeclaration prepared by France and Saudi Arabia, which Finland has joined.

https://yle.fi/a/74-20183645

2025-09-18

A third-country national is someone from outside the EU who needs a residence permit to live, study, or work in Finland. Citizens of EU/EEA countries and Switzerland do not require a permit to enter Finland and are not included in the report. In 2024, two-thirds of students granted residence permits for higher education were citizens of South Asian countries. Almost half of students with residence permits are enrolled in technology or ICT programs, a much higher share than among other students.
