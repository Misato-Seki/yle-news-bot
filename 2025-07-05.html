Grim Saturday in eastern Finland: Three traffic deaths, two suspected drownings

https://yle.fi/a/74-20171218

2025-07-05

Two middle-aged men are believed to have drowned after a boating accident early Saturday on Lake Haapajärvi. Five adults were in a small aluminium rowboat designed for four, venturing out in very windy conditions late at night. All were under the influence of alcohol and none were wearing life vests. The search was called off on Saturday afternoon, as authorities said that the underwater visibility was too murky for divers to work.
