Heatwave warms inland waters to record-high temperatures

https://yle.fi/a/74-20174938

2025-07-29

Temperatures in North Savo's Lake Pielavesi and the Pielisjoki River reached record highs last week. Tuesday marked the 18th day in a row of temperatures exceeding 30 degrees somewhere in Finland. That outpaced the 1972 record of 13 days of 30-plus-degree weather, which had stood since data collection began in 1961. According to the FMI, the warm weather is set to continue this week, including at night.

Nearly 20 dead seals found along Finland's south coast

https://yle.fi/a/74-20174919

2025-07-29

15 dead seals were discovered along the shores of the Pellinge islands in Porvoo. Another four dead seals found on islands around the same time in nearby Loviisa. As of Tuesday, the cause of the mass deaths remained a mystery. The worst-case scenario would be a serious disease outbreak, such as avian influenza. No cases of bird flu or large numbers of dead birds have been observed in the area this summer.

Helsinki goes a full year without a traffic death

https://yle.fi/a/74-20174831

2025-07-29

Helsinki has not recorded a single traffic fatality in the past 12 months. The city's most recent fatal accident occurred in early July 2024 on Keinulaudantie in the city's Kontula district. In the past year, there were 277 injury-causing traffic accidents in Helsinki — a sharp contrast to the nearly 1,000 injurious accidents recorded annually in the late 1980s.
