Finland braces for weekend of thunderstorms and rain showers

https://yle.fi/a/74-20182594

2025-09-12

A low pressure front from the west on Friday brought scattered rain showers to the Helsinki metropolitan area and along the southwest coast. The precipitation also means cooler temperatures in the southern part of the country, with a high of 15 degrees Celsius expected in the capital region. Saturday will likely see thunderstorms and strong winds in central and southern parts, with temperatures again hovering around 15-20 degrees Celsius. There is also more rain on the horizon for next week, meteorologist Matti Huutonen said.

Bloomberg: Finland considers floating Posti on stock exchange

https://yle.fi/a/74-20182536

2025-09-12

Finland's national mail carrier Posti might be subject to an initial public offering (IPO) as early as this year. Bloomberg estimates that the sale would earn the Finnish state between 100 and 150 million euros. The idea of allowing the general public to buy and sell ownership in Posti is nothing new. The government ofJuha Sipilä(Cen)investigated the optionin 2016, but ultimately decided against it.
