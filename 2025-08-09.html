Smoke from extensive Canadian wildfires is expected to reach Finland on Saturday evening. Most of the smoke will remain high in the atmosphere and will not affect air quality. In some areas, the sunset may appear more intensely red than usual, caused by smoke particles scattering the sun's rays. The smoke has already spread over southern and central Sweden earlier in the day, the Finnish Meteorological Institute says.
