Holiday home sales up 10% this summer, prices well above pre-Covid era

https://yle.fi/a/74-20177896

2025-08-17

Almost 10 percent more cottages and cabins have been sold this year in May-July than in the same period last year. A total of 560 holiday homes were sold in July, nearly all of them pre-owned, the federation said. In July, the largest number of vacation-home deals were closed in Southwest Finland, a total of 60, it said. The average price for a vacation property is 130,000 euros this year, compared to 128,000 euro last year, according to the Federation of Real Estate Agencies.

"Putin cannot be trusted," Finland and Nordic-Baltic Eight nations warn

https://yle.fi/a/74-20177887

2025-08-17

Finland joins the Nordic-Baltic Eight nations in a joint statement supporting Ukraine's sovereignty and a just end to Russia's full-scale war. The statement unequivocally calls for a ceasefire, "credible security guarantees," the release of Ukrainian children abducted by Russia, and commitments from Ukraine's allies to prevent future Russian attacks. Finland, Norway and the three Baltic countries, which share borders with Russia, have been among the strongest supporters of Ukrainian sovereignty.
