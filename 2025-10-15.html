Survey: Russian attitudes toward Finland split along generational lines

https://yle.fi/a/74-20188575

2025-10-15

A survey commissioned by Finland's embassy in Moscow has found a clear generational divide in how Russians see Finland. Younger people more likely to hold a positive view. Pensioners were generally far more negative. The embassy has carried out similar surveys since 2017, and while opinions have hardened over the long term, they have stabilised over the past three years. Overall, 38 percent of Russian respondents said in the latest poll that they viewed Finland in a positive light, down from 40 percent last year.
