Cold snap brings deep frost, snowfall and traffic warnings across Finland

2025-11-16

In Lapland, the mercury dropped to around –20C overnight on Saturday. North Ostrobothnia and Kainuu saw roughly 10 centimetres of snow. Scattered snow showers were also reported across southern and central Finland. Traffic weather warning for Sunday covering North Savo, North Karelia, Kainu and western parts of North O Strobothnia. Snow and sleet are expected to create poor driving conditions, slowing traffic.

Reindeer stroll through downtown Rovaniemi, delighting tourists

https://yle.fi/a/74-20194390

2025-11-16

Four reindeer were spotted in central Rovaniemi, Finnish Lapland's largest city. The animals are not typically seen in the city's busiest downtown area. Reindeer are semi-nomadic animals that roam freely but are privately owned and managed by local herding cooperatives. Local herders, who are trained in capturing and handling the animals, can transport them away in a van if necessary.

Europe's largest outdoor artificial ice field in Helsinki reopens after renovation

https://yle.fi/a/74-20194383

2025-11-16

Helsinki's Oulunkylä artificial ice field reopens to the public on Sunday. The overhaul included upgrades to the ice field itself, the refrigeration technology of the adjacent ice hall and the maintenance building. Free public skating sessions will be held daily, with separate areas reserved for skaters and stick users. Artificial ice facilities across Helsinki will open gradually through November and December depending on weather conditions.
