Electric cars fare worse in Finnish car inspections – up to half of Teslas flunk

https://yle.fi/a/74-20186654

2025-10-04

Finnish transport agency Traficom says that EVs are more likely to fail mandatory inspections than gasoline and diesel passenger cars of the same age. Cars from one company – Tesla – are largely responsible for the relatively high overall EV test-failure rate. Kia Niro, Volvo XC40, Volkswagen Golf and Hyundai Ioniq, all had failure rates of just four percent.
