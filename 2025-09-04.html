Finland plans €30m programme to boost hiring of young adults

https://yle.fi/a/74-20181172

2025-09-04

The Finnish government is planning a 30-million-euro voucher programme as an incentive to employers to hire people between the ages of 18 and 29 for a period of at least six months. The programme will be particularly aimed at young adults with little education and little — or no — job experience. Details of the programme are still being considered, and the sum of each voucher has not been announced yet.
