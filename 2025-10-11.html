HBL: Finland's longest-serving MP may never return to Parliament

https://yle.fi/a/74-20187894

2025-10-11

Ben Zyskowicz of the National Coalition Party became the longest-serving MP in Finnish history on Friday. The 71-year-old suffered a stroke in late July, and is on sick leave until at least the end of January. As of Friday, he had been in Parliament for a historic 17,003 days, or about 46 years. His wife of 43 years is Muslim, a member of Finland’s small Tatar community.

Finnish Hornet fighters join Nato nuclear exercise

https://yle.fi/a/74-20187874

2025-10-11

Four Finnish Hornet fighters will take part in Nato's annual nuclear weapons exercise starting on Monday. Minister of DefenceAntti Häkkänen confirmed that Finnish fighter jets and staff officers will be joining the manoeuvres. The exercise, dubbed Steadfast Noon, involves 71 aircraft from 14 countries, including the United States and Germany. This is the second time that Finnish jets are participating in the exercise – but the first time it has said so in advance.

Finns' confidence in the future drops

https://yle.fi/a/74-20187849

2025-10-11

Finns' confidence in their own future is declining, according to the latest state-run Kansalaispulssi (Citizens’ Pulse) survey. Young people aged 15–29 were less confident about their future, with only 56 percent saying they were quite or very confident. More respondents pointed to employment among the key themes that the government should focus on. Healthcare is still seen as the most important theme, just ahead of employment – as Finland now has Europe'ssecond-highestjobless rate.
