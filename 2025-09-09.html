2025-09-09

Cases of rhinovirus, parainfluenza virus, adenovirus and coronavirus (Covid) infections have been reported in the national infectious disease register. Because people usually manage to deal with their illness at home, there are no accurate statistics about which viruses are currently most prominent. Only very few of these types of viral infections end up being reported to the national register.

Border Guard: Foreign national illegally entered Finland from Russia

https://yle.fi/a/74-20181920

2025-09-09

The Finnish Border Guard suspects that a foreign national illegally crossed into Finland from Russia on Monday. The crossing took place in the Välivaara area, near the eastern border town of Kitee. Finnish border authorities have remained tight-lipped about the suspect's age, nationality, gender and general background — but confirmed that the individual has applied for asylum in Finland. The Border Guard has launched a preliminary investigation into the incident, and the suspect is currently in the custody of Finnish authorities.

Finnish firms recruit fewer employees from abroad, survey finds

https://yle.fi/a/74-20181858

2025-09-09

Last year, 37 percent of responding companies said they had hired an international professional in the past five years. This year, that proportion fell to just 28 percent. The drop of nine percentage points compared to last year is likely due to the weak economic situation. The decline was most pronounced in the retail sector, where only 15.5 percent of respondents reported hiring foreign professionals, down from 27 percent last year.
