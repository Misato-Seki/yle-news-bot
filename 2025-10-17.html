MPs broadly back government in confidence vote over employment policies

2025-10-17

The government of PMPetteri Orpo(NCP) won a vote of confidence in Parliament on Friday. The vote was 92–71, with 36 MPs absent. The interpellation question, submitted by the Centre Party and the one-man Movement Now, condemned the government for failing in its employment policy. The parties cited figures from Statistics Finland, showing that the nation's unemployment rate has soared to 10 percent.

Finland sees sharp rise in reports of sexual offences, assaults and shoplifting

https://yle.fi/a/74-20188907

2025-10-17

There has been a notable increase in the number of suspected sexual offences reported to authorities during the first nine months of this year. According to data released by Statistics Finland on Friday, a total of 7,800 suspected sex offences were reported between January and September. A total of 408,900 offences against the Criminal Code were recorded by the police, customs officials and the Finnish Border Guard from January to September this year, a 3.1 percent increase on the same period of 2024.

2025-10-17

Finnish state railway company VR is trying to improve the reception of calls and mobile data on trains. The move follows last year’s much-criticised effort to boost Wi-Fi and phone reception on board. On Friday, the company unveiled its latest plan, saying it will laser-engrave train windows as a means of facilitating access to internet base stations as trains roll past. VR says it will lasers 10,000 windows over the next two years, starting in November.

Life expectancy in Finland rises to record-high level

https://yle.fi/a/74-20188875

2025-10-17

The life expectancy for girls born in Finland last year is 84.8 years, while for boys it is 79.6 years. The latest figures mark a steep increase in life expectancy in Finland compared to the mid-1970s, when the equivalent rates were 75.4 for girls and 66.9 for boys. The data also revealed that women live longer than men in every region across the country, but the largest gender gap was in Kainuu.
