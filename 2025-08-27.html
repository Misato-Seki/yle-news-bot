New car sales slump to 30-year low, EV share rises

https://yle.fi/a/74-20179709

2025-08-27

From January through July, new passenger car registrations in Finland fell by 3.8 percent compared with the same period of last year. That makes 2025 so far the worst year for sales in three decades, the Finnish Information Centre of the Automobile Sector said. The Finnish car market is among the weakest in Europe, it said. Last year, only 13 new passenger cars were registered in Finland per thousand inhabitants, which is the fifth worst figure in Europe.

Experts worried by rise in corporal punishment of children

https://yle.fi/a/74-20179620

2025-08-27

Survey by Central Union for Child Welfare shows that corporal punishment is still used quite frequently in Finland. In general, adult attitudes towards violent forms of discipline have become more negative over the decades. But the latest poll suggests a reversal of this positive trend, the NGO says. Forty-one percent of respondents said they have used some form of disciplinary violence in raising their children. Nearly 30 percent said they had grabbed a child roughly or shoved them.

Finland to reintroduce landmines in January

https://yle.fi/a/74-20179625

2025-08-27

Finnish Defence Forces will reintroduce anti-personnel mines as early as January next year. Finlandwithdrew from the Ottawa Anti-Personnel Landmines Convention in July. The six-month waiting period for exiting the mine-ban agreement ends on 10 January. The Defence Forces previously manufactured its own mines, but going forward, they aim to procure them from domestic defence industry suppliers.
