Lowering the average age of cars would require Finns to buy significantly more new vehicles. In June, 7,449 new passenger cars were registered in Finland, which is 10.1 percent more than in June last year.

https://yle.fi/a/74-20172002

2025-07-10

The birch pollen season in Finland has come to an end. The sneezy season started in late March and only concluded early this month. The University of Turku started monitoring Finland's airborne pollen counts in 1974. Up to one-fifth of people in Finland are sensitive to birch allergy, which is the most common source of pollen allergies. The Finnish Allergy, Skin and Asthma Federation warned thatclimate change was prolonging Finland's allergy seasons.
