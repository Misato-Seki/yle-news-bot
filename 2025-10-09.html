Finland's benefits agency has reported that its expenditure on unemployment benefits amounted to four billion euros in 2024. It said the spending figure reflected a five percent increase over the previous year. Finland wants to tighten rules on child home care benefits

https://yle.fi/a/74-20187503

2025-10-09

The Ministry of Social Affairs and Health has opened consultations on amendments to the Child Home Care Allowance Act. The aim is to link eligibility to a mandated period of residence. Under the proposal, families would qualify for the allowance only after both parents — or, in single-parent households, the resident parent — have lived in Finland for at least three years. The reform, modelled on Norway's system, would apply only to families moving to Finland after the law takes effect.
