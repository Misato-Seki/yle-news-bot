Finnish schools hire more teachers as reforms bring changes to learning support

https://yle.fi/a/74-20176189

2025-08-06

A new law amendment reforming how learning support is provided in early childhood education, elementary schools, and upper secondary schools came into force this month. The 2025–2026 academic year serves as a transition period for these changes. As part of the reform, schools are required to hire more teachers and invest more time and money. The reform may look different across schools due to varying current practices. Some schools had already hired additional teachers in March, while others had not.
