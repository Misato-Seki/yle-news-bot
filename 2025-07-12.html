Former Wagner fighter seeks asylum in Finland after fleeing Russia

2025-07-12

A former Wagner Group fighter is in the custody of Finnish authorities after illegally crossing the border into Finland and applying for asylum. The man, known by his first nameYevgeny, was apprehended by the North Karelia Border Guard on 17 June near the town of Kitee. He has publicly identified himself as a Wagner soldier on social media and recently posted videos harshly criticising Russia's military leadership.

Police fatally shoot knife-wielding man on Helsinki rooftop

https://yle.fi/a/74-20172353

2025-07-12

A man has died after police used a firearm during an operation in the Alppiharju district of Helsinki on Saturday morning. Officers were called to a residential building after reports of a man carrying a bladed weapon in a stairwell. The man fled to the roof upon their arrival and allegedly moved toward officers despite repeated commands to stop. Police initially attempted to subdue him using a taser, but when that failed, an officer opened fire.

Hot and sunny Saturday across Finland, but thunderstorms expected in the south

https://yle.fi/a/74-20172326

2025-07-12

Hottest conditions are forecast in central Finland, where temperatures could climb to 30 degrees Celsius. Even in the north, conditions will remain dry and pleasant. But in parts of southern Finland, heavy showers and thunderstorms are expected to develop by midday. These could briefly cool temperatures to around 20 degrees. On Sunday, the weather is set to remain largely similar, with even warmer conditions reaching southern parts of the country.
