Bloomberg: Germany orders up to €5b worth of armoured vehicles from Finnish defence firm Patria

https://yle.fi/a/74-20175147

2025-07-30

Germany plans to buy around 3,500 armoured vehicles from Finnish defence contractor Patria. The deal is intended to replace Germany's aging Fuchs armoured vehicles. Patria has manufactured its eight-wheeled AMV armoured vehicles in Finland since 2004. Germany joined the framework agreement for the multinational Common Armoured Vehicle System (CAVS) project earlier this year. This facilitates vehicle orders from Patria, which is half-owned by the Finnish state.
