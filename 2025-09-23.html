Wolt concludes restructuring talks with no redundancies announced

Finnish delivery firm Wolt has announced the completion of restructuring talks with staff representatives. The restructuring affected employees working for Wolt's customer service unit in Finland. Finnish Orthodox Church begins restructuring talks

https://yle.fi/a/74-20184606

2025-09-23

The Finnish Orthodox Church has announced the beginning of restructuring talks with staff representatives. The church said the talks will include workers attached to its service centre, its seminary, as well as the staff of all dioceses. But bishops will not be included in the negotiations. The statement added that the church needs to find savings following a decision by the Finnish government to cut its funding. It is mainly funded by member taxes, but support from the Finnish state accounts for about 35 percent of its income.

Finnish government wants to steer social assistance recipients toward other forms of support

https://yle.fi/a/74-20184547

2025-09-23

The government aims to more firmly direct applicants for social assistance to instead apply for primary benefits such as unemployment or student financial aid. Social assistance is distributed by the Social Insurance Institution (Kela) Under the new legislation, Kela must check each social assistance applicant to see whether they have applied for any other form of benefit. If they have not done so, and fail to do so despite Kela's request, the basic part of their social assistance would be slashed by 50 percent.

Highest 10% of earners to see average tax cut of over €2,000 next year

https://yle.fi/a/74-20184498

2025-09-23

The Ministry of Finance has published its calculations of the effects of the government's budget proposal for next year on individual taxes. Based on the tax changes, disposable income will only decrease for those in the lowest income tenth percentile. Relatively and numerically, the income of the top tenth percentile will improve the most. Taxes on tobacco, alcohol and soft drinks will increase, while a reduction in fuel tax will increase disposable income for those who drive combustion-engine vehicles.

Finland reports bleakest unemployment figures since 2009

https://yle.fi/a/74-20184490

2025-09-23

Unemployed people in Finland rose to 267,000 during August, according to the latestLabour Force Survey. This is an increase of 23,000 people from the situation in August last year, pushing the number over the quarter-of-a-million mark. In August, the unemployment rate itself — meaning the proportion of unemployed people in the labour force — was 9.3 percent. The unemployment rate trend for people aged 15 to 74 — which SF adjusts for seasonal and random variation — climbed to 10 percent.
