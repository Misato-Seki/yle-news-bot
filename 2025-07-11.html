WWF calls on Finland to halve seal hunting quota

https://yle.fi/a/74-20172262

2025-07-11

WWF Finland has called on the government to cut the proposed hunting quota for Baltic ringed seals by half. Seal hunting is permitted in Finland for both grey seals and BalticRinged seals, with quotas set by the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry. The Baltic Ringed seal is considered to be an endangered species as it is heavily dependent on snow and ice, meaning that global warming is a serious threat to its survival.

Finland formally withdraws from Ottawa landmine treaty

https://yle.fi/a/74-20172189

2025-07-11

Finland has submitted its intention to withdraw from the Ottawa Anti-Personnel Landmines Convention. The withdrawal will take effect from January 2026, the Ministry for Foreign Affairs confirmed. Finland signed the treaty in 2012, but the governmentbegan preparations to withdraw earlier this year, citing increased security concerns. This followed similar moves by countries such as Estonia, Lithuania, Latvia and Poland, the foreign ministry said.
