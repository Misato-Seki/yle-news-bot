Finland re-starts processing of Syrian asylum applications

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185283

2025-09-26

Finnish Immigration Service (Migri) has resumed processing of asylum applications from Syrians. Those applications have been in limbo since December due to dramatic changes in the security situation in Syria. Asylum seekers whose applications are approved may be granted either refugee status, subsidiary protection or residence permits on other grounds. Those receiving negative decisions are generally given 30 days to leave Finland voluntarily, Migri warns.

Kuopio’s rising immigration strains integration services in a challenging job market

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185217

2025-09-26

Kuopio's population growth in recent years has been driven largely by immigration. Ukrainians make up the biggest group, along with arrivals from Russia and Estonia. Labour-based migration has also increased, and local universities are attracting more international students. Many arrivals also turn to NGOs for support, such as Kompassi, an international meeting place run by the settlement association Puijola. The organisation offers free Finnish language lessons and groups on a first-come, first-served basis.

This is peak season for elk accidents, warns Fintraffic

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185168

2025-09-26

Fintraffic Road Traffic Centre urges motorists to be wary of elk crossing roads at this time of year. There were over 1,650 elk collisions in Finland last year, up by almost 10 percent from the previous two years. Most elk accidents occur at dawn and dusk in the autumn. Northern Ostrobothnia, including the Oulu region, recorded the most elk-related crashes with 163.
