Enersense is selling its subsidiary Enersense Offshore to Canadian-owned shipbuilding group Davie. The deal, announced Tuesday, is valued at approximately 7.5 million euros. Helsinki Shipyard will become the only specialised shipbuilder in Finland with an independent capacity to deliver vessels from design to final delivery.
