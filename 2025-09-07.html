Fresh research points to troubling levels of racism in schools

https://yle.fi/a/74-20181522

2025-09-07

Researchers found that four out of five non-white pupils had encountered racism. Pupils often felt they had received little emotional support after experiencing racism. Two-thirds of teachers said they believed there was structural racism in schools. Four-year project was funded by the Academy of Finland and examined 14 classes across seven lower secondary schools in southern Finland. The research unfolds against a backdrop of controversial public remarks about immigration by leading politicians.

Russians still moving to Finland despite border closure

https://yle.fi/a/74-20181505

2025-09-07

By July, 355 Russians had been granted residence permits this year for study purposes. A further 300 were granted permits for work. Applications have, however, dropped sharply compared with 2019. The number of first-time study-based applications has halved, while work-based Applications have fallen by almost as much. The government gradually closed the eastern border in stages in late 2023. It is suspected that Russia was pushing migrantsto the checkpoints to put pressure on Finnish authorities.

Lunar eclipse visible on Sunday evening

https://yle.fi/a/74-20181494

2025-09-07

A total lunar eclipse will be visible across Finland on Sunday evening, weather permitting. Because the moon will be low on the horizon, the best views will be from open, elevated spots. No equipment is needed as the eclipse can be seen with the naked eye. During the eclipse, the moon moves into the Earth's shadow. Earth's atmosphere bends sunlight around its edges, casting a reddish glow onto the lunar surface.
