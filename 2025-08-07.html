Syke: Finland's cyanobacteria levels low for this time of year

https://yle.fi/a/74-20176363

2025-08-07

Cyanobacteria, or blue-green algae, situation has calmed in Finland. Fewer sightings reported on inland waters and along the coast than is typical for this time of year. High concentrations of algae have only been recorded in a few select regions. Cyanobacteria can be toxic and at times deadly to humans. The amount of blue- green algae typically increases in lakes with late summer rains.

Perseid meteor shower visible over Finland next week

https://yle.fi/a/74-20176344

2025-08-07

Perseid meteor shower will be especially visible over some parts of Finland on the nights between 10 and 14 August. The Perseid is generally only visible south of the city of Oulu, as the bright 'nightless nights' of Finland's north significantly affect visibility. Ursa advises people to find an open area away from the light pollution of built up areas if they want to catch a glimpse of the meteor showers.

FMI: July was exceptionally warm, especially in Finnish Lapland

https://yle.fi/a/74-20176284

2025-08-07

The month of July was "exceptionally warm" this year in Finland and neighbouring countries. Globally, last month was the third warmest July ever recorded. Turkey set a new national heat record when the mercury soared to 50.5 degrees Celsius at the end of July. Finland also set a heatwave record last month, with at least one weather station recording temperatures above 30 degrees Celsius for 22 consecutive days.
