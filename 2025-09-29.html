Finland plans video streaming fees to benefit domestic productions

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185653

2025-09-29

Finnish government has set up a working group to establish an arrangement that would obligate video streaming platforms to pay fees to operate in the country. The funds from those obligatory payments would be directed towards the production of domestic films and TV shows. Similar obligatory payment arrangements are already in place in 17 EU countries, including Denmark and Norway. The Finnish government plans to present a legislative proposal about the matter next year.

Helsinki Metro service to Vuosaari resumes after bridge overhaul

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185582

2025-09-29

Helsinki's Metro line east of Itäkeskus station returned to service on Monday. Work on the Vuosaari bridge, an effort which began in May, has been completed. Thousands of commuters were directed to buses or had to find other means of transportation. Metro traffic will be interrupted again in 2026-2027 for a six-month period when a bridge between Sörnäinen and Kalasatama will be repaired.

Finnish consumer confidence remains low, mortgages still seen as risky

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185550

2025-09-29

Rising unemployment and global political uncertainty are shaping Finnish people's views on homeownership. One in three considers a mortgage a financial risk, according to a recent S-Bank survey. High housing costs and economic uncertainty particularly worry young people under 35. Finns’ outlook for the country’s overall economic development improved slightly in September compared with both the previous month and a year earlier.
