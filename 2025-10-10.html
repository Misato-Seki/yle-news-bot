Finland moves to introduce tuition fees for non-EU upper secondary students

https://yle.fi/a/74-20187777

2025-10-10

Finland will stop paying municipalities to take in upper secondary students from outside the EEA. Non-EU students will be required to pay in order to attend general upper secondary as well as vocational schools. News comes as a blow to many small rural high schools in northern and eastern Finland that have relied on foreign students to stay open. Ending state subsidies for foreign students would save the government around two million euros. The legislative changes are set to take effect on 1 August 2026.

More Ukrainians plan to make Finland home

https://yle.fi/a/74-20187708

2025-10-10

Only six percent of Ukrainians intend to return home once the war ends. Sixty-six percent of respondents said they no longer intend to go back to Ukraine. A further 26 percent have yet to decide on a possible return. Ukrainians who fled to Finland after Russia's invasion of their country face an uncertain future as theirtemporary protection status is due to expire in March 2027. The survey received responses from just over 2,000 individuals, the majority of whom came from eastern Ukraine.
