Finland's wolf population rises by 46% within a year

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185995

2025-10-01

Natural Resources Institute Finland estimates that there were 413–465 wolves in Finland last March, with the most likely number around 430. In the spring of 2024, the population was estimated at 277–321, most likely around 295 individuals. The strongest growth in wolf numbers was seen in western Finland, especially the southwest. Wolves sometimes prey on livestock and pets, but have not killed a human in Finland since 1882.
