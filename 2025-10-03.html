Finnish conscripts to begin anti-personnel landmine training next year

https://yle.fi/a/74-20186570

2025-10-03

The Finnish Defence Forces (FDF) plans to gradually begin training conscripts in the use of anti-personnel mines next year. The move follows Finland's withdrawal from the Ottawa landmine treaty earlier this year. Finland joined the Ottawa Treaty in 2012, but increasing tensions betweenRussia and the Westprompted it to withdraw from the agreement. The National Defence Training Association of Finland is preparing to offer landmine training, in case the FDF requests it.

Tampere garage fire started with hybrid car that was likely being charged

https://yle.fi/a/74-20186482

2025-10-03

Fires in parking garages are relatively rare in Finland. The country has seen a total of 16 fires at such facilities over the past decade. However so far this year, there have been six parking garage fires, including Wednesday's incident in Tampere and another on Thursday night in Kerava. In Kerava the vehicle that caught fire was an electric car that was being charged, according to local authorities.

Finnish court: We have no jurisdiction in Eagle S case

https://yle.fi/a/74-20186464

2025-10-03

Helsinki District Court has confirmed that it does not have jurisdiction in the case of the Eagle S oil tanker, which authorities have suspected of involvement in the severing of subsea cables. Prosecutors had alleged that the crew allowed the ship's massive anchor to drag across the sea floor, breaking five undersea cables, including a key electricity link between Finland and Estonia. The criminaltrial of three crew members beganat the end of August. The prosecutor had demanded a minimum of 2.5-year jail terms.
