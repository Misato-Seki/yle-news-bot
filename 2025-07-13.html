Deal reached in Finnish aviation labour dispute, strikes called off

https://yle.fi/a/74-20172437

2025-07-13

Finnish Aviation Union (IAU) and Service Sector Employers Palta reach a deal. All industrial actions scheduled between 16 and 25 July have been cancelled. The new collective agreement will last approximately 1.5 years. IAU ChairJuhani Haapasaari said he is pleased that a compromise has been reached in a difficult and long labour dispute. The parties will review the impact of earlier cost-cutting agreements on employees' earnings.

Rainy start to summer brings early mushroom boom in southern Finland

https://yle.fi/a/74-20172427

2025-07-13

Mushroom expert Tuija Biskop says the season is off to a strong start. In the southern half of Finland, mushrooms are appearing earlier than usual. Despite the promising start, it’s too early to declare this year an exceptional one for mushrooms. To enjoy a long mushroom season, we’ll need steady rainfall well into the autumn, Bisko says.

Finland records hottest day of the summer as temperatures break heat threshold

https://yle.fi/a/74-20172417

2025-07-13

New seasonal high measured in Kaskinen's Sälgrund, where temperature rose to 31.5 degrees Celsius on Sunday afternoon. This reading surpassed the previous high of 30.3 degrees, which had been set just a day earlier on Saturday at Joensuu's Linnunlahti weather station. Before this weekend, the summer's top reading had been 29.0 degrees, recorded in Jämsä on 15 June. Finland is currently under a mass of very warm air, resulting in widespread heatwave conditions.
