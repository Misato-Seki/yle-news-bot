Finnish MP opens up about her sex work in Helsingin Sanomat interview

https://yle.fi/a/74-20181453

2025-09-06

Anna Kontula, a Left Alliance parliamentarian from Pirkanmaa, says she engaged in sex work in her youth. She sold sex at the age of 16, while still a student, citing both financial difficulties and a search for excitement. In Finland, the selling and buying of sex is legal, unless the trade involves pimping or victims of human trafficking. She has served as an MP since 2011 and said that her current term will be her last in the legislature.

SVT: Over 120,000 flights hit by Russian signal interference in Baltic region this year

https://yle.fi/a/74-20181449

2025-09-06

Nearly 123,000 flights in Sweden and neighbouring countries have been affected by signal interference during the first four months of this year. Signal interference affects GPS positioning and other satellite systems. It can cause navigation errors and even system malfunctions during flight. Authorities have been able to trace the source of the interference to Russia and have urged airlines to raise their preparedness in response to the growing signal interference.

https://yle.fi/a/74-20181431

2025-09-06

Fraudsters' ads circulating on social media have used the names and photos of Yle journalists to trick people into "investing" through bogus platforms. Over the summer, a screenshot masquerading as an Iltalehti news article spread across social media, reaching tens of thousands of Finns on Facebook and Instagram.
