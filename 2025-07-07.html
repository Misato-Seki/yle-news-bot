More rain coming, but Finland will warm up soon

https://yle.fi/a/74-20171499

2025-07-07

A major precipitation system will reach Finland's southern areas on Tuesday. Most of the rainfall will be seen in the south and particularly in south-eastern areas. Once that precipitation system moves on, weather conditions are expected to be warm and sunny. Temperatures exceeding 25 degrees Celsius — the threshold for 'hot' weather in Finland — are possible over the weekend, but conditions will also make thunderstorms likely.

Student family immigration rises sharply, interior minister criticises reform

https://yle.fi/a/74-20171418

2025-07-07

In 2021 there were around 1,300 applications filed by students from outside the EU. Last year the figure had grown to more than 12,000 for first-time and extension applications. This year, the number is expected to reach a record 15,000. For the first time, applications from student family members have outpaced those from workers' families. The increase follows policy changes introduced by Prime MinisterSanna Marin's (SDP) government in 2022.

2025-07-07

Finnair has cancelled around 100 flights on Monday as a result of industrial action by the Aviation Union. Originally, the airline had planned to operate about 330 flights. Mixed-gender barracks now standard in Finnish Defence Forces, feedback largely positive

https://yle.fi/a/74-20171359

2025-07-07

Mixed-gender rooms have been available to all interested conscripts in Finland. The policy has become an established practice with little negative feedback. Two corporals serving in the Jaeger Brigade in Rovaniemi in Finnish Lapland had nothing negative to say about the experience. Men in Finland have a legal obligation to complete either military or civilian service, while women have the option to volunteer for the military.
