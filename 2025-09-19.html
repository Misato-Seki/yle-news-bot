Opposition parties challenge Finnish government over Palestine

https://yle.fi/a/74-20183986

2025-09-19

Three of Finland’s four main opposition parties have filed an interpellation over the government's failure to act in recognising Palestinian statehood. Next week's UN General Assembly meeting would be the right time to recognise Palestine, the opposition parties charged in a statement on Friday. Two of the four governing parties, the Finns Party and the Christian Democrats, oppose the recognition of Palestine. So far, nearly 150 countries – more than three-quarters of all UN members – have recognised Palestine.

Aviation union fined for walkout at Helsinki Airport

https://yle.fi/a/74-20183922

2025-09-19

The Finnish Labour Court has ruled that a walkout by members of the Aviation Union (IAU) last December was illegal. The Labour Court ordered the IAU and its three chapters to pay a fine of 67,000 euros. The walkout onDecember 2halted nearly all air traffic at Helsinki Airport, Finland’s main aviation hub. The union organised a string of further strikes during the spring and summer after the collective agreement for the sector had expired.

Government announces plans to list Posti on the stock exchange

https://yle.fi/a/74-20183873

2025-09-19

The Finnish government has confirmed its plans to list national mail carrier Posti on the Helsinki stock exchange. As part of the planned listing, the state would sell some of its shares, and Posti would issue shares to its employees. According to Posti, the goal of the listing is to broaden its ownership base, strengthen its brand and increase financial flexibility. The state intends to remain the majority shareholder.

Friday's papers: Overseas shopping frenzy, home maintenance matters, and panda update

https://yle.fi/a/74-20183839

2025-09-19

The CEO of retail giant S Group sounds an alarm over rising overseas package deliveries. Hannu Krook warns that the market operates under double standards, with minimal oversight of legal requirements for overseas sellers. Domestic companies must ensure product safety and conduct audits, which increases their costs and gives third-country e-commerce operators a competitive advantage. Finnish retailers are bound by producer responsibility, which requires them to manage the recycling and disposal of imported materials responsibly.
