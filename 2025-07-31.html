Finland's foreigner removals up by 30%, news group reports

https://yle.fi/a/74-20175183

2025-07-31

Finnish police removed more than 1,350 foreigners from the country during the first half of 2025. Those removed were mostly from Estonia and Romania. The growth in removals was prompted by increased enforcement of immigration law, one of Prime MinisterPetteri Orpo's (NCP) government programme goals. Authorities can issue a removal order following a preliminary criminal investigation, sending individuals back to their home country and banning their return.
