Many tourism firms in Finnish Lapland violate labour laws, say union and hospitality association

https://yle.fi/a/74-20192902

2025-11-08

There are a growing number of firms operating in northern Finland’s tourism sector that disregard labour laws and general ethical standard. Service Union United PAM and the Finnish Hospitality Association (MaRa) say many employees have been illegally required by employers to take part in unpaid training sessions. One Asian employee, for instance, had an employment contract starting in mid-October but was told she had to arrive in Rovaniemi, the regional capital, two weeks earlier.

Earthquake jolts southeast Finland

https://yle.fi/a/74-20192850

2025-11-08

A minor earthquake rattled Kotka, southeast Finland, around 11pm on Friday. According to the University of Helsinki’s Seismological Institute, the temblor had a magnitude of 1.2. Earthquakes occur relatively frequently in the Kymenlaakso region, which also includes the towns of Kouvola and Hamina. For example, in December 2022, a 1.8 magnitude earthquake was felt in Kotka.
