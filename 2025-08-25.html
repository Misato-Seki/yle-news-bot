2025-08-25

2025-08-25

The study involved nearly 400 young adults, of whom about 14 percent were in a high-risk group. The World Health Organisation (WHO) identified problematic social media Use as a public health challenge back in 2015. It estimates that up to a quarter of the world's population suffers from harmful use of digital media.

THL: Government cuts pushing more children into poverty than previously estimated

https://yle.fi/a/74-20179145

2025-08-25

Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare (THL) published detailed assessments of the impacts of budget cuts by the government. Families with children who receive public assistance will be hardest hit. THL's new estimate takes into account the combined effects of the government's tightening of unemployment benefits and housing allowances since 2024. In Finland someone is officially defined as poor or low-income if their income falls below 60 percent of the median monthly income.
