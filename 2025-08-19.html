Crisis helpline answers record number of calls over summer

https://yle.fi/a/74-20178324

2025-08-19

Mieli Mental Health Finland's Crisis Hotline has received a record number of calls this summer. In June and July, the association responded to nearly 20,800 calls, an increase of 23 percent from the previous summer. There were about 670 discussions via Mieli’s crisis chat service in June and June. The organisation has received feedback that some such calls may have saved the life of suicidal callers.

Finnish Defence Ministry launches programme to boost fitness of incoming conscripts

https://yle.fi/a/74-20178237

2025-08-19

Finnish Ministry of Defence launches scheme to improve physical fitness of young men before they enter compulsory military service. Programme will begin this autumn at conscription events in five municipalities before expanding nationwide by 2027. Experts from regional sports organisations will provide guidance on exercise, as well as information on local opportunities for physical activity. In Paimio, Lahti and Kouvola, recruits will also use the MarsMars fitness app, which includes the Defence Forces' recently published fitness programme.
