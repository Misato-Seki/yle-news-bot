Finland testing new ways to involve kids and youth in decision-making

https://yle.fi/a/74-20192574

2025-11-06

Finnish government ministries are working to consider the opinions and voices of children and young people in their work. The Ministry of Justice has launched a trial effort to form panels of young citizens to help guide the ministries. The project will see the establishment of two citizens' panels, one for children and the other for youth. The goal is to fill the panels with "as representative and diverse a group of Finnish children and youth as possible"

Immigrants are good for Finland's public finances, new study shows

https://yle.fi/a/74-20192556

2025-11-06

Immigrants are a net benefit to Finland's public finances, according to a fresh study. Foreign-language speakers account forabout 10 percentof the population, but they only account for about five percent of income transfers. In 2023, they paid some 2.7 billion euros in taxes while receiving 2.4 billion euro in transfers. For speakers of native Finnish languages, the corresponding figure was five billion euros.

Finns maintain trust in tax system despite dip in public sector confidence, survey finds

https://yle.fi/a/74-20192539

2025-11-06

A majority of Finns have a positive attitude about paying taxes, according to a new survey commissioned by the country's Tax Administration. More than 80 percent of respondents said they believe that tax evasion is 'always' wrong. A majority also said they see the Tax Administration as a trustworthy authority. On the other hand, the survey found people having an increasingly negative attitude about the agency, compared to a similar poll in 2021.

https://yle.fi/a/74-20192501

2025-11-06

Three Polish men sentenced to prison terms of just over two years for aggravated theft. The convictions stem from a series of bombings of automated teller machines (ATMs) that the men carried out in late 2024 and early this year. The trio destroyed ATMs in three towns: Vaala in North Ostrobothnia as well as Vaasa and Eura on the west coast. Altogether, they managed to steal almost 200,000 euros.

Finland approves cash-for-clunkers bill

https://yle.fi/a/74-20192497

2025-11-06

The Finnish Parliament has approved a law that will allow the state to pay rebates to people who turn in their old cars and buy new, lower-emission vehicles. Thursday's vote was 124-40, with 35 MPs absent. The programme, commonly referred to as a 'cash-for-clunkers' arrangement, enables new car buyers to apply for the rebates right away. To qualify, the used vehicles must be at least 10 years old and taken to a recycling centre.

2025-11-06

Yle's latest political support poll shows support for the biggest parties remaining unusually steady from a month earlier.
