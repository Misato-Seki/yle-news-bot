Oulu court finds no racist motive in stabbing of high school student

https://yle.fi/a/74-20171874

2025-07-09

Oulu District Court has handed down its verdict in the case of a man accused of stabbing a foreign-background high school student in the city last September. In its ruling, the court found the defendant not guilty of attempted murder as a psychiatric assessment had previously found he was not criminally responsible. The court ruled that the defendant will continue to be held against his own will at a psychiatric unit. The victim sustained life-threatening injuries, but survived after receiving prompt medical treatment.

Alko's sales drop by 10% in June

https://yle.fi/a/74-20171806

2025-07-09

Sales at Finland's alcohol monopoly Alko dropped by 10 percent in June, compared to the same month last year. Alko's wine sales fell by 12.5 percent, while other products — like mixed drinks, beer and cider — fell by 2.6 percent. The firm attributed the sales decline to law reforms that were implemented last summer, enabling grocery stores and other shops to sellfermented beverages.
