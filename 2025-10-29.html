THL: Kids in Finland increasingly skip school lunches

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190876

2025-10-29

Only slightly more than half of lower secondary school students eat the free school lunch every day. The survey also found that teenage girls in particular were more likely to skip school lunches. Finland became the first country in the world to provide all schoolkids daily, free-of-charge lunches in 1948. The initiative was aimed at tackling poverty-related child malnutrition in the post-war years, and has since become a proud cornerstone of the Finnish education system.
