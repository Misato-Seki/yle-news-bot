Valtonen: Hamas must be disarmed, by force if necessary

https://yle.fi/a/74-20186734

2025-10-05

Foreign Minister Elina Valtonen says she is hopeful that a ceasefire will be reached in Gaza. On Friday, US PresidentDonald Trumpcalled on Israel to stop its attacks. According to Gaza health authorities, about 70 Palestinians have been killed in Israeli attacks since Friday. A few days earlier, Trump unveiled a 20-point peace plan, which Valtonens has welcomed. She stressed that the extremist group Hamas must be removed from Palestine and its administration, and that it must be disarmed.

Kotka to Derry: Stubb approves Finnish leg of European route E16

https://yle.fi/a/74-20186722

2025-10-05

European motor route 16 (E16) to be extended across Finland. The new stretch will be from Rauma on Finland’s west coast to Kotka in the southeast. A year ago,Finland proposedto the UN Economic Commission for Europe that the highway be extended through Finland. Its segment will be some 400 kilometres long, making it the second-longest national section of the route.

STT: Estonian firm that exploited Ukrainian builders also fined for hiding data on foreign workers at Rauma shipyard

https://yle.fi/a/74-20186686

2025-10-05

A network of shady Estonian companies involved in the exploitation of Ukrainian shipyard and construction workers have also operated at the Rauma shipyard, Finnish news agency STT reports. It cites documents from regional administrative agencies indicating the companies provided workers to the shipyard at least in 2021–22. Earlier this year, the Regional State Administrative Agency (Avi) for Southwest Finland imposed a fine on one of the Estonian firms.
