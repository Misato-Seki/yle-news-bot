What's happening to Finland's lakes and rivers? Urgent action needed to protect declining species, researchers say

https://yle.fi/a/74-20191697

2025-11-02

Finnish Environment Institute releases first-ever snapshot of biodiversity in Finnish lakes and streams. Report warns that the biodiversity of inland waters has significantly declined. In areas affected by forestry, the worst situation is in small streams in Central and Eastern Finland. Study also surveyed mosses and other aquatic plants as well as phytoplankton, diatoms and other algae in streams and lakes.

October was unusually mild and damp – and November is set to be, too

https://yle.fi/a/74-20191727

2025-11-02

October was mild throughout Finland, especially in the north. The month’s average temperature ranged between 1.5 degrees Celsius in Enontekiö in the far northwest, to 10 degrees in the southwestern archipelago. Northern parts of the country received the most rain, with the border town of Tornio collecting more than 116 millimetres of precipitation. The European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) predicts mild weather through November, according to commercial forecaster Foreca.
