Cases in Helsinki increased by roughly 50 percent in the first part of the year compared with the same period last year. Not all incidents result in police reports, though the reporting threshold has fallen. Those caught shoplifting tend to suffer from substance abuse problems, according to the police. More fathers taking parental leave

https://yle.fi/a/74-20192976

2025-11-09

Family leave reform that took effect in August 2022 has increased fathers' use of parental leave. Under the reform, parental leave is in principle divided equally between both parents. Between January and September, fathers accounted for over 22 percent of all parental allowance days — two percentage points more than during the same period last year. The autonomous Åland Islands differ notably from mainland Finland. During the first nine months of this year, fathers there used more than a quarter of all parents' leave days.
