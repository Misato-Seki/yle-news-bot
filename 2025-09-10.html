Finnish manufacturing firms' order books swelled by more than 22 percent in July compared to a year earlier. Finland sees fewer workplace accidents this year

https://yle.fi/a/74-20182117

2025-09-10

Workplaces in Finland will see a four percent decline in work-related accidents this year, compared to 2024. The most significant decrease will be seen in trips to and from work, which are expected to decline by 13 percent. Up to 20 percent of those mishaps occurred in Helsinki last year, according to the Finnish Workers' Compensation Center (TVK) TVK will publish the final occupational accident figures and related data for this year in February 2026.

https://yle.fi/a/74-20182098

2025-09-10

More than half of highly educated immigrant women in Finland feel that the country's societal climate has deteriorated in recent years. The survey queried almost 300 people, including women from abroad who live in Finland. Some of the women who claimed the societal climate situation had worsened blamed government policies as well as "exclusionary attitudes in the labour market" The survey was commissioned by the Finnish Confederation of Professionals (STTK)
