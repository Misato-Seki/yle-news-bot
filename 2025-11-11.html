Finnish tax office to step up cryptocurrency profit tracking

https://yle.fi/a/74-20193456

2025-11-11

Around 450,000 taxpayers in Finland hold some form of cryptocurrency. There is no law on the books that makes reporting cryptocurrency ownership obligatory, but profits from their sale must be declared. Currently only about 10 percent of cryptocurrency sales get reported to the Tax Administration, according to the agency's senior advisor,Juho Hasa. When the new regulations come into effect at the beginning of next year, it will become easier for authorities to track profits from virtual currency transactions.
