Court cancels bear hunting permits

https://yle.fi/a/74-20177551

2025-08-14

The Administrative Court of Eastern Finland issued an interim decision on Thursday prohibiting the implementation of all exceptional permits granted by the Finnish Wildlife Agency. Thirteen permits had been granted, allowing the killing of 129 bears in the eastern population management area beginning on 20 August. According to the administrative court, the injunction was necessary to ensure that effective legal protection was not jeopardised. The Administrative Court said that it will issue final decisions on the appeals towards the end of this year or early next year.

Finland's inflation rate drops to 0.2 percent

https://yle.fi/a/74-20177546

2025-08-14

The year-on-year change in consumer prices fell to just 0.2 percent in July, according to preliminary data published on Thursday by Statistics Finland. The slight overall rise in prices last month was driven by the increased cost of general hospital fees, coffee and wireless telephone services. These hikes were however somewhat mitigated by reductions in the prices of electricity, the average interest rate on housing loans and interest rates on consumer credit.
