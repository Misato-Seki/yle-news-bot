Farming profits improve

2025-09-28

Average entrepreneurial income is expected to reach just over 37,000 euros this year. The most profitable operations are poultry and cattle farms, along with large greenhouse businesses specialising in vegetables. Smaller farms typically lag behind their larger counterparts in earnings. In the early 2000s, Finland had some 100,000 farms, but todayless than half of that number remains, according to the Natural Resources Institute Finland (Luke)

Finland looks to trim welfare for immigrants

https://yle.fi/a/74-20185399

2025-09-28

Finance Minister Riikka Purra says "integration allowance" will replace unemployment support for some immigrants. The benefit could be pared back to the level of basic social assistance, a reduction from the current jobseekers' benefit. Such a shift, championed by the Finns Party, would require amending the constitution, a change the party deems essential. The draft is expected to be sent out for consultation later this year.
