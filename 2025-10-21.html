Comet Lemmon now visible with naked eye over Finland

https://yle.fi/a/74-20189506

2025-10-21

Comet Lemmon can now be seen in the Finnish sky even with the naked eye, according to the astronomical association Ursa. The best time to observe the comet is between now and approximately 5 November – weather conditions permitting. In November, the comet will pass the Sun, and its orbit will change slightly. Next time Lemmon returns to the inner solar system is about 1,150 years from now. It last passed close to the Sun 1,350 years ago.

Unemployment rose in September

https://yle.fi/a/74-20189502

2025-10-21

The number of unemployed rose to around 259,000 in September, up 32,000 from a year earlier, according to Statistics Finland. Employment among men rose by 30,000, while the number of employed women fell by 14,000 compared with September 2024. The unemployment trend rate, or long-term direction, remained high at 9.9 per cent in September. In August, the trend rate surpassed 10 percent, marking one of the bleakest readings in years.
