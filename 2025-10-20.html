Police: Swedish criminals running drug network in Finland

https://yle.fi/a/74-20189349

2025-10-20

Helsinki police say they have uncovered a large-scale narcotics operation run from Sweden. The network is suspected of importing and distributing drugs across Finland. The operation turned out to be part of a wider network that channelled drugs from the capital to other parts of Finland and into several European countries. Thirty-five people are suspected of involvement in the crimes. Most are, or have been, in custody, and several have been detained in absentia.

Bankruptcies rise again

https://yle.fi/a/74-20189314

2025-10-20

Nearly 290 firms filed for bankruptcy last month, 15 more than in September 2024. The construction sector accounted for the largest share of filings, followed by retail and manufacturing. The sharpest growth came from agriculture, forestry and fisheries, where 14 firms filed. Eastern Finland and the construction sector have borne the brunt of the Ukraine war's worsening economic fallout, according to arecent SME poll. The companies placed under bankruptcy proceedings employed the equivalent of more than 1,070 person-years.

Lying down more dangerous to health than intermittent sitting, Finnish study finds

https://yle.fi/a/74-20189311

2025-10-20

Sitting down for more than 20 minutes at a time was found to be associated with bigger waistlines. Intermittent sitting means getting up for a little stroll every 20-30 minutes. Benefits were seen in walks that involved something as simple as going to the kitchen to get a cup of coffee. On the other hand, lying down and lounging during waking hours was a contributing factor for heart disease.
