Bear shot in Salo

https://yle.fi/a/74-20173410

2025-07-19

The bear had been sighted in the yards of homes in the city of Salo. It also seemed to have been attracted to a beehive in the local area. The animal was considered to pose a serious danger to public safety. Sightings of bears near residential areas have become more common in recent years as Finland's bear population hassteadily increased. The bear's remains will be sent to the Finnish Food Authority for further examination.

2025-07-19

A man in his 40s has been detained by police on the Åland Islands on suspicion of killing one person and attempting to kill two others. According to local media reports, one person died and three others — including the suspect — were injured following aviolent incident at a cottage in Lumparland. All four people involved in the incident are believed to be Swedish citizens. Homicides are rare on the Åland islands.

Finnish court orders deportation of mother, two children held in detention unit for 55 days

https://yle.fi/a/74-20173327

2025-07-19

Nigerian woman and her two young children have no legal grounds to remain in Finland. Family had been held at a detention facility in Joutseno, near the city of Lappeenranta, for almost two months. Under Finnish law, the detention of a child must only be used as a last-resort measure, and the period of detention should be as short as possible. Family's extended period of confinement drew widespread criticism.
