Finland's Saimaa ringed seal populations continue to grow

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190679

2025-10-27

Population estimate for the rare mammals this year stands at 530 individuals. In 2024, the seal population was estimated at 495 individuals. Saimaa ringed seals are among the most endangered seals in the world. The population estimates are based on the number of individuals observed during the month of January, a count which does not includepups born in the spring. The situation in the northern part of Lake SaimAA is concerning, according to Metsähallitus.

Many Finnish workplaces overly reliant on 'nonsense' personality tests, researchers say

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190593

2025-10-27

Pseudo-scientific personality or self-assessment tests have grown rapidly at Finnish workplaces and recruitment companies. Inkeri Koskinen, a philosopher of science and Academy Research Fellow at the University of Helsinki, has found that workplace tests are often pseudoscience and lack a scientific foundation. The tests and associated tools are seen by many companies as a means of tackling various problems — from finding a suitable interview candidate to addressing low morale.

Finland's young drivers' licence reforms move forward

https://yle.fi/a/74-20190597

2025-10-27

Ministry of Transport and Communications is inviting comments on a draft proposal to amend the Driving Licence Act. The aim is to ensure that exemptions are only granted to a young person's "continuous and essential need for mobility", while also improving safety on the roads. Finland's legal driving age is 18, but a law change in 2018 allowed 17-year-olds to obtain driving licencesunder certain, less-stringent circumstances.
